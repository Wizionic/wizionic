# Install as a Progressive Web App

A **Progressive Web App (PWA)** is this website installed like an app: an icon on your home screen or Dock, and its own window without the usual browser toolbar.

On a phone, tablet, or Mac this is the usual way to keep Wizionic handy. Windows and Linux can use the native Wizionic app instead.

Chat, notes, and other data still live **on this device** after you install. Signing in with the same account lets other devices sync.

## Android {#android}

Use **Chrome** when you can. Other Android browsers work too.

### Chrome

1. Open this site in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Tap **Install**.

Chrome may also show an **Install** bar at the bottom of the page. Use that if you see it.

### Samsung Internet

1. Open this site in **Samsung Internet**.
2. Tap the menu (usually **☰** at the bottom).
3. Tap **Add page to** → **Home screen**.
4. Confirm.

### Firefox

1. Open this site in **Firefox**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Confirm.

After install, open **Wizionic** from your app drawer or home screen.

## iPhone {#ios}

On iPhone you must use **Safari**. Chrome, Firefox, and Edge on iOS cannot add a real home-screen app.

1. Open this site in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll the actions and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

Open **Wizionic** from the home screen. It runs full screen, without Safari’s address bar.

If you do not see **Add to Home Screen**, swipe the share sheet up — it is often below the first row of icons.

## iPad {#ipad}

Same idea as iPhone: use **Safari**, not Chrome.

1. Open this site in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** in the toolbar (square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

On a larger iPad the Share button is in the top toolbar. After install, Wizionic appears on the Home Screen and in the App Library.

## Mac {#mac}

### Safari (macOS Sonoma and later)

1. Open this site in **Safari**.
2. Choose **File → Add to Dock…** (some versions say **Add to Dock** from the Share button in the toolbar).
3. Confirm the name **Wizionic**.

The app appears in the Dock and in Launchpad. It opens in its own window.

### Chrome

1. Open this site in **Chrome**.
2. Click the **install** icon in the address bar (a computer with a down arrow), or open **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install Wizionic…**.
3. Click **Install**.

Chrome puts Wizionic in your Applications folder (Chrome Apps) and may pin it to the Dock.

### Edge

1. Open this site in **Edge**.
2. Click the **app available** icon in the address bar, or **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**.
3. Click **Install**.

## Chromebook {#chromeos}

1. Open this site in **Chrome**.
2. Click **Install** in the address bar, or **⋮ → Install Wizionic…**.
3. Confirm.

Wizionic then appears in the launcher and can be pinned to the shelf.
