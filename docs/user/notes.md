# Notes

Notebooks and entries live on this device. Bodies are encrypted; titles may be cleartext so the sidebar can list them quickly.

## Basics

- Create a notebook, then add entries. The editor is rich text (Quill).
- Click or tap an entry to edit. The check mark or **Ctrl+S** (**Cmd+S** on Mac) saves and closes the editor.
- While editing, the stars button on the toolbar opens Chat with that note available to the AI. Summaries stay in chat until you explicitly ask to save or overwrite the notebook.
- The mic on the toolbar dictates into the entry. It stays on until you click it again or click outside the note. Pick the speech-to-text model under [Settings → Voice](/help/settings-voice).
- On the desktop app, that same session also saves compressed audio on this device (not synced). Hover a phrase and click to play from that moment, or use a timestamp chip. A trash button on the player removes the recording but keeps the text. Deleting the notebook also deletes its audio from this device.
- A pause of about three seconds starts a new paragraph. You can also press Enter while recording to move the cursor.
- Search at the top of the notebook list. **Ask my notes** uses your current Chat model when one is selected.
- The last notebook you opened is remembered on this device.
- Note paper and default text follow the app theme. Colors you apply in the editor stay as you set them.
- Drag to reorder when the UI allows it.
- Sign in if you want the same notebooks on another device via [sync](/help/sync).

## Password-protected notebooks

If you set an **account password**, you can lock a notebook. Unlock asks for that password only — not email or SMS 2FA.

Protected notebooks cannot be read or edited by chat tools until you unlock them in the UI.

## Chat tools

When Notes tools are listed, the model can search notes, list notebooks, add an entry, or append to one. It overwrites an existing entry only when you explicitly ask it to save. It cannot invent a notebook password. Protected notebooks stay out of search and tools until you unlock them.
